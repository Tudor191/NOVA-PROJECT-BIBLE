## Relationship to the Phase 4 series

Phase 4B is the second of Phase 4's six milestones (4A–4F) and the one master
scope §5 calls *"the milestone that makes Phase 3 visible."* It is the first point
at which `planning-engine`, `reasoning-engine`, `capability-engine` and
`action-engine` — built across Phases 3A–3D and until now reachable only from
other engines — are readable from a browser through the approved boundaries.

It does not, however, satisfy the criterion that measures that visibility. AC-3
asks for those four engines to be *exercised* from the browser, not merely
*observed* from it, and 4B built the observation surface without the two controls
(capability install, an end-to-end approval) and without the provider that
generation requires. The distinction between surfacing Phase 3 and exercising it
is the whole of this milestone's NO-GO.

**Phase 4A's own Gate Review and Project Health record do not exist.** That gap is
recorded here (field 21) and analysed against the protocol in the Gate Review
§13.1, which finds it does not gate 4B. It is carried forward so `phase-4`'s
eventual closure inherits a complete list rather than rediscovering it — the same
discipline [`README.md`](README.md) and protocol §0.2 apply to Slice ledgers.

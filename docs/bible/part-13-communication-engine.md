True communication requires understanding.

Timing.

Context.

Intent.

Feedback.

Adaptation.

The purpose of the Communication Engine is to create natural, continuous and intelligent interaction between NOVA and the user.

Every conversation.

Every notification.

Every visual update.

Every spoken response.

Every alert.

Every recommendation.

Every confirmation.

Flows through this system.
